Description Ingredients Delivery & Returns

Our Lavender Dream bar is made the slow way — cold-process saponified, hand-cut and left to cure for four weeks before it ever reaches you. We blend British lavender essential oil with a little chamomile for a gentle, relaxing scent that lingers without overpowering.

Because every batch is made fresh by hand, no two bars are exactly alike. Colour and marbling will vary slightly from the photo. This is a seasonal, limited-run bar and may sell out.

Key Ingredients
Saponified olive, coconut & shea oils, lavender essential oil, chamomile extract, mica (colour). Weight approx. 100g. Vegan & palm-oil free.
Care
Keep your bar on a well-drained dish between uses so it lasts longer. Best used within 3 months.
Handmade Promise
Every Smush bar is cut, wrapped and labelled by hand in our Bristol workshop — never mass produced.
