Our Story
Handmade in Bristol since 2014
It started on a market stall

Smush began in 2014 with a single fold-out table at St Nicholas Market, a stack of hand-cut soap bars and a hand-painted sign. People kept coming back — for the smell, for the chat, and for soap that felt nothing like the supermarket stuff.

Today we make everything by hand in small batches in our Bristol workshop. We’ve grown to three little high-street shops, but the way we make things hasn’t changed: simple ingredients, no plastic, nothing tested on animals, and a friendly face behind the counter.

We’re still a small team that knows its regulars by name — and we’d like to bring a bit of that to everyone, wherever you are.

Meet the Founder
“We just wanted to make soap we’d actually want to use.”

Smush is run by Rosie and a small team of makers. Rosie started mixing soap in her kitchen, selling to friends, then to the market, then — somehow — to three shops across Bristol and Bath.

Every bar is still cut, cured and wrapped by hand. Nothing leaves the workshop that we wouldn’t put on our own skin.

— Rosie, Founder
